How Loona Audio Tools Work

  1. Choose a tool and add your audio. Your file is read by your own browser - nothing is sent anywhere. The Voice Recorder only asks for microphone permission when you press Record.
  2. Cut, join or record. Drag the waveform handles, arrange your files, or speak - everything happens on your device.
  3. Download the result. The finished audio is created on your device and downloads instantly.

Why People Trust Browser-Based Tools

Most online audio sites ask you to upload your songs, lectures and voice recordings to a stranger's server. Loona Audio Tools are built the opposite way: the processing code comes to your audio, not the other way round. That means faster results, works-anywhere privacy, and nothing of yours stored, logged or shared. Close the tab and every trace of your audio is gone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Loona Audio Tools really free?

Yes - every tool is 100% free, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no daily limits imposed by us.

Are my audio files uploaded to your server?

Never. Every tool runs 100% in your browser using the Web Audio technology built into it. Your files and recordings stay on your device from start to finish.

Which audio formats can I use?

The cutter and joiner accept the formats your browser can decode - MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG in all modern browsers. Exported files are standard WAV, which plays everywhere.

Does the Voice Recorder send my voice anywhere?

No. The recorder runs entirely on your device. Your microphone is only switched on when you press Record, and the recording is only saved if you choose to download it.

Which devices are supported?

Any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer - Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work. The Voice Recorder needs a microphone and permission to use it.
