About Loona Audio Tools

In short: Loona Audio Tools is a free set of 3 audio tools by Loona Automation that runs entirely in your browser - your audio is never uploaded anywhere.

What is Loona Audio Tools?

Loona Audio Tools is a free collection of 3 online tools for everyday audio work: an Audio Cutter that trims MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG files with a visual waveform, an Audio Joiner that combines several audio files into one track, and a Voice Recorder that records straight from your microphone.

It is part of Loona Automation, a family of free, problem-solving web tools built for students, teachers, creators, offices and small businesses - in Pakistan and around the world.

Our Difference: Privacy by Design

Almost every audio website works by uploading your files to a server. We built the opposite: all processing happens inside your own browser with JavaScript and the Web Audio API. Your audio is never transmitted, never stored and never heard by us. The Voice Recorder only switches your microphone on when you press Record, and only for as long as you are recording. It is faster, it works on any device, and it is simply the respectful way to handle people's recordings.

Our Mission

Loona Automation's mission - "Bolti Duniya" - is to make useful technology speak everyone's language: free tools that solve real problems, without sign-ups, paywalls or fine print. If a tool saves you five minutes today, it has done its job.
