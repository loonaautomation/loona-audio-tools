Privacy Policy

In short: your audio never leaves your device - every tool works 100% inside your browser, and this page explains exactly what that means and what analytics we use.

The Short Version

Your audio never leaves your device. Every Loona Audio Tools operation - cutting, joining and recording - is performed 100% locally in your web browser using JavaScript. We have no upload server for your audio, we cannot hear it, and nothing about its content is collected, stored or shared.

What Happens to Your Audio

  • Files you choose are read into your browser's memory only, processed on your device, and offered back to you as a download.
  • When you close or refresh the tab, the audio data in memory is discarded by your browser.
  • We never receive, store, back up or analyse your audio files or voice recordings.

Microphone Access (Voice Recorder)

  • The Voice Recorder asks your browser for microphone permission only when you press the Record button - never on page load.
  • Your microphone is live only while you are recording. When you press Stop, the microphone is switched off.
  • The recording exists only in your browser's memory until you choose to download it. If you close the tab without downloading, it is gone.
  • Nothing you record is transmitted to us or to any other server by this site.

Analytics and Advertising

Like most free websites, we use privacy-respecting measurement and advertising services that may collect anonymous usage data (such as pages visited and device type) through cookies or similar technologies:

  • Google Analytics - helps us understand which tools are popular so we can improve them.
  • Microsoft Clarity - shows us anonymous interaction patterns (clicks and scrolls) so we can fix confusing layouts.
  • Google AdSense - displays advertisements that keep the tools free; Google may use cookies, including DoubleClick cookies, to personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

These services never receive the audio you process or record - that never leaves your device. You can block or clear cookies at any time in your browser settings; the tools will still work.

Your Choices

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and message solely to reply. We do not sell personal information. To ask anything about this policy, write to loonaautomation@gmail.com.
