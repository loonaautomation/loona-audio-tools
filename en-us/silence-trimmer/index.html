Silence Trimmer - Remove Silence From Audio Online Free

In short: the Silence Trimmer removes the quiet stretch at the start and end of a recording - it shows you exactly how many seconds of silence it found at each end before you download the trimmed WAV, completely free, and your file never leaves your device.

Almost every phone and microphone recording starts with a few seconds of fumbling silence and ends the same way. This tool measures those silent ends honestly - in seconds, at a threshold you control - and cuts them off, keeping a small safety padding so the first and last words are never clipped.

How to use this tool

  1. Click the box above or drag your audio file into it - MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG all work.
  2. Read the analysis: how much silence was found at the start, how much at the end, and how much audio you keep.
  3. Adjust the threshold if needed - lower (like -60 dB) removes only true silence, higher (like -30 dB) also removes quiet room tone - and set how much padding to keep.
  4. Click "Download Trimmed WAV", then listen to the trimmed file in the player to check the ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Silence Trimmer free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit imposed by us.

Are my audio files uploaded to a server?

No. Your file is decoded and trimmed 100% inside your browser with the Web Audio API. It never leaves your device.

What does the dB threshold mean?

It is the level below which sound counts as silence. -40 dB (recommended for voice) treats anything quieter than 1% of full volume as silence. -60 dB removes only near-perfect digital silence, while -20 dB is aggressive and can eat quiet speech endings.

Will my first or last word get cut off?

The padding setting keeps extra audio at both ends - 0.1 seconds by default - so natural word starts and fade-outs survive. If a recording has very soft beginnings, raise the padding to 0.3-0.5 seconds.

Does it remove silence in the middle of the file?

No. This tool trims only the start and the end. Pauses in the middle are part of the recording and are left exactly as they are.

How it works

Your file is opened right inside your browser and decoded into raw sound samples with the Web Audio API - nothing is uploaded to any server. The tool scans from the start until it finds the first sample louder than your threshold, and from the end backwards to the last loud sample. Everything outside those two points is the silence being removed; the padding you choose is added back on each side.

Because the measurement works on the actual samples, the numbers shown - silence at start, silence at end, seconds kept - are exact for the threshold you picked, not estimates. Change the threshold and the analysis updates immediately so you can see the trade-off before downloading.

Worked example

You record a 3-minute lesson on your phone: 4 seconds of silence before you start speaking and 6 seconds at the end while you reach for the stop button. The analysis shows exactly that. With the -40 dB threshold and 0.1 seconds of padding, you download a clean 2 minute 50 second WAV that starts and ends with your voice.

More questions

Why does the tool say my whole file is silence?

Every sample in the file is below the chosen threshold - either the recording really is silent or extremely quiet, or the threshold is set too high for it. Lower the threshold to -50 or -60 dB and the analysis will run again.

I want to remove a pause in the middle too. How?

Cut the file into pieces with the Audio Cutter, keeping the parts before and after the pause, then join them back together with the Audio Joiner.

Why does the download come out as WAV?

WAV is the format your browser can create natively without extra software, and it keeps the audio exactly as decoded. Use the Audio Converter if you need the trimmed file as MP3 afterwards.
