Voice Recorder - Record Your Voice Online Free

In short: the Voice Recorder records your voice straight from your microphone, entirely on your device - free, private, and nothing is uploaded, ever.

A quick voice note, a lesson explanation for your class, a practice take for a song or a message in your own words - press Record, speak, and download your recording. Your microphone is only switched on while you record, and the recording stays on your device unless you choose to save it.

Press Record when you are ready. Your browser will ask for microphone permission - it is only asked for when you press the button.

Private by design: your recording is made and kept 100% on your device. The microphone is requested only when you press Record, it is switched off the moment you press Stop, and your voice is never uploaded to a server, never stored by us and never heard by us.

How to record your voice

  1. Press the Record button above - your browser will ask for microphone permission, so allow it.
  2. Speak normally; the timer shows how long you have been recording. You can Pause and Resume if you need a break.
  3. Press Stop, then listen back with the player that appears.
  4. Download the recording to keep it - or Delete it and record a fresh take.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Voice Recorder free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up and no recording limit imposed by us.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Your voice is recorded and kept only in your browser's memory on your own device. It is saved only if you press the download button - nothing is ever sent to a server.

When is my microphone switched on?

Only after you press Record and give permission. The moment you press Stop, the microphone is switched off again.

What format is the recording?

Your browser records in its standard web audio format (usually WebM). It plays in all modern browsers and apps, and can be converted to MP3 or WAV with the Audio Cutter or any converter.

What happens if I close the tab before downloading?

The recording is lost, because it only exists in the tab's memory. Always download your recording before closing the page.

How it works

When you press Record, the page asks your browser - not a server - for access to your microphone. Your browser captures your voice using its built-in MediaRecorder technology and keeps the sound in the memory of this tab only. Pressing Stop switches the microphone off immediately and turns the captured sound into a playable recording, still entirely on your device.

Worked example

A teacher wants to send her class a short spoken explanation of tomorrow's lesson. She opens this page on her phone, presses Record, speaks for about a minute, presses Stop, listens once to check, and downloads the file to share with her students - without installing any app and without her voice ever passing through anyone's server.

Because nothing is uploaded, recording works even on a slow connection, and there is no account, inbox or cloud folder where your voice could be kept without you knowing.

More questions

Why did the microphone not start?

Your browser needs microphone permission for this site. Check the lock or settings icon in the address bar, allow the microphone, then press Record again. On some phones the permission prompt appears the first time only.

Can I listen to the recording before saving it?

Yes - as soon as you press Stop, a player appears with your recording so you can listen back before deciding to download or delete it.

How long can I record?

There is no limit set by this tool. Very long recordings use more of your device's memory, so for sessions longer than an hour, downloading in parts is safer.

Can I record on my phone?

Yes - any phone browser with microphone access works. Keep the tab open and the screen on while recording for the best result.
