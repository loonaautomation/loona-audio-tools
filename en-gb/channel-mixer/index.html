Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Channel Mixer free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit imposed by us.

Are my audio files uploaded to a server?

No. Your file is decoded and converted 100% inside your browser with the Web Audio API. It never leaves your device.

How does Stereo to Mono work?

Every pair of left and right samples is averaged into a single sample. The result plays identically in both ears. Sounds that exist only as a difference between the channels can become quieter - that is a property of stereo itself, explained honestly in the hint text on the page.

Does Mono to Stereo make the sound richer?

No - it copies the same signal to both channels, so the file becomes a proper stereo file that plays centred in both ears, but no new stereo information is created. That is exactly what most players and upload forms need.

My recording only plays in one ear. Which option fixes it?

Left Channel Only if the sound is in the left ear, or Right Channel Only if it is in the right ear. The working channel is copied to both sides, so the recording plays centred in both ears.

How it works

Your file is opened right inside your browser and decoded into separate left and right sample streams with the Web Audio API - nothing is uploaded to any server. Each conversion is simple, exact sample maths: mono averages the channels sample by sample, stereo duplicates, swap exchanges them, and the one-sided modes copy the chosen channel over the other. The result is written as a standard WAV file at the original sample rate.

Because the maths is exact, nothing is re-compressed and nothing is guessed: what you hear in the preview is precisely what the downloaded file contains.

Worked example

A teacher records an interview with a stereo microphone, but the guest sat on the side the faulty cable muted - the guest is only audible in the left ear. She chooses Left Channel Only, and the downloaded WAV plays the guest centred in both ears, ready to share with her class.

More questions

Can I separate a stereo song back into vocals and instruments?

No - that is not a channel operation. Once sounds are mixed into two channels, channel tools can only mix, copy or swap those channels, not un-mix the original sounds.

What happens when I swap channels on a mono file?

Nothing changes - there is only one channel, so there is nothing to swap. The tool passes the file through unchanged as mono WAV.

Why does the download come out as WAV?

WAV is the format your browser can create natively without extra software, and it keeps the converted channels exactly as computed. Use the Audio Converter if you need MP3 afterwards.
