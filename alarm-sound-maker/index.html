About this tool

An alarm needs a sound that wakes you without shocking you: a 30-second clip with a gentle start works better than a sudden blast. This page trims any audio to the section you want, pre-set to 30 seconds.

Pick a section with a clear beginning (a fade-in helps), export it, and set it as the alarm tone in your clock app. Android clock apps accept MP3 files directly.

Worked example

A user trims the opening 30 seconds of a calm piano piece and sets it as her weekday alarm — she wakes to music instead of a siren.

Frequently asked questions

Should the alarm fade in?

Yes — a 2–3 second fade-in is kinder and still wakes you. Use the Fade Editor on this site after trimming, if your alarm needs it.

Can I use a song as an alarm?

On Android, yes — any MP3 can be an alarm tone. iPhones need the file added via iTunes/Finder, which is a different workflow.
